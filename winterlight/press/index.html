Winterlight is a cosy Christmas seek-and-find game for ages 3 to 8, made by G2G, a small independent studio. Explore 32 hand-drawn scenes and find 12 hidden treasures in each one. No ads, no accounts, no data collected, and it works offline.

Quick facts

  • Ages 3 to 8 (Little Elves mode for ages 3 to 6)
  • 32 scenes: 3 free forever, 29 more with a one-time purchase
  • No ads · no accounts · no tracking · works offline
  • Languages: English, Português (BR), Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad and Android
  • Purchases are protected by a grown-ups-only question

Free printable Christmas sheets

Six scenes with 12 treasures to find in each, plus an answers page. A4, ready to print. Free to share with families and classrooms.
